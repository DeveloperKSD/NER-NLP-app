Tim Cook, the CEO of Apple Inc., met Narendra Modi in New Delhi, India on Monday, 15 January 2024, to discuss a $2 billion investment in the Nasdaq-listed company's new factory. The deal, covering roughly 10,000 jobs and a 3% stake, was announced first in English and later in French. Analysts at Goldman Sachs, a leading American bank, raised their price target by 20% to $250 per share ahead of the second quarter earnings call.

Mahatma Gandhi was born on 2 October 1869 in Porbandar, Gujarat, and later studied at University College London before leading India's independence movement against the British Empire; India gained independence in 1947, and the Nobel Peace Prize committee is still criticized for never honoring him.

Lionel Messi, an Argentine footballer, scored twice as Inter Miami beat the Los Angeles Galaxy 3-1 at Chase Stadium in Fort Lauderdale on Saturday evening, five days before their Club World Cup match against Real Madrid; the win, Miami's third in a row, drew a crowd of nearly 21,000 fans.

Elon Musk founded SpaceX in 2002, acquired Twitter for $44 billion in October 2022, and now lives in Austin, Texas; his company Tesla unveiled the Cybertruck at an event covered widely by CNN and the New York Times, and he was named Time's Person of the Year that December.

The Eiffel Tower, located near the Seine River in Paris, attracts over 6 million visitors annually and is taller than the Statue of Liberty; nearby, the Mona Lisa hangs in the Louvre Museum. Mount Everest, on the border of Nepal and China, remains the world's tallest mountain at 8,849 meters.

Under the Clean Air Act, the World Health Organization and the United Nations warned that global temperatures could rise by 1.5 degrees Celsius, costing economies billions of dollars by the end of the century. Microsoft, Google, and Amazon are each investing heavily in generative AI, with one report estimating the market could reach a trillion dollars by 2030.
